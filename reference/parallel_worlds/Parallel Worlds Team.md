Yes—several members of the Parallel Worlds team stayed active in the ALttP hacking scene, and in some cases they even collaborated again on later projects. The most notable post-Parallel Worlds work is Conker's High Rule Tail, which was explicitly described as starting as a "spiritual sequel to Parallel Worlds" before evolving into its own thing.

Here’s what each credited contributor has done since.
🧭 Euclid (Original Hacking, Dungeon Design, ASM)

Euclid remained active as both a hacker and toolmaker.

    Zelda3 Parallel Remodel (2012): A rebalanced and reworked version of the original Parallel Worlds, intended to address its notorious difficulty.

    BS Zelda Map 1 & 2 Restorations: Hacking work restoring the original BS Zelda maps.

    Tower of the Triforce: Euclid's original demo, which was the foundational concept that eventually became Parallel Worlds. It was later picked up and finished by another modder, Letterbomb.

    Tooling: He also released the Bszelda 3 AST Dungeon Editor and a Zelda 1 Dungeon Editor.

🎨 SePH (Overworld Design, GFX Editing)

SePH continued to lead major visual and structural hacks.

    Zelda3 Goddess of Wisdom (2011): A complete hack where he served as the main overworld designer.

    The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Islands: A hack that actually started as Lyra Islands in 2004/2005 before he abandoned it and later worked with Euclid on Parallel Worlds.

    The Legend of Zelda - A Link to the Proto: A complete hack released in 2020.

    Conker's High Rule Tail: SePH was the lead hacker for this project, handling overworlds, dungeons, graphics, and storyline.

⚙️ Con (ASM Work for v1.2)

Con's ASM contributions were foundational for the team's later, more ambitious project.

    Zelda3 IQ Test: A puzzle-focused hack of ALttP released by Con in 2016.

    Conker's High Rule Tail: Con was part of the core "dreamteam" on this project, which featured extensive new ASM for menus, HUD elements, and HDMA effects.

    Conn's Ultimate ASM Pack: A publicly shared pack of ASM patches he worked on with PuzzleDude and others.

🔧 qwertymodo (Supervisor for v1.2, ASM Insertion)

qwertymodo became a key figure in the technical and preservation side of the community.

    A Link to the Past DX: A major hack he co-developed with Conn, which later served as the foundation for the popular Redux hack.

    MSU-1 Support: He created an ASM hack to implement MSU-1 functionality in Zelda 3, enabling CD-quality soundtracks. He also contributed MSU-1 support for Parallel Worlds Remodel.

    10-Year Anniversary Update: He oversaw a major rebalancing and feature update for Parallel Worlds itself.

🧩 PuzzleDude (Supervisor for v1.23, Beta Testing)

PuzzleDude carved out a niche in puzzle and challenge hacks, while also supervising remodels.

    Zelda3 Parallel Remodel: He was the remodel supervisor for the 2012 rebalanced version of Parallel Worlds.

    Zelda3 IQ Test Series: He released several puzzle-oriented hacks, including Zelda3 IQ test Remodel and Zelda3 IQ test Revenge of the Einstein.

    Utility Hacks: He also released smaller improvement hacks like Zelda3 HP hack and Zelda3 God Mode.

    Conker's High Rule Tail: He was part of the core team on this project as well.

💎 The Reunion: Conker's High Rule Tail

The most direct answer to "have they made anything else together" is Conker's High Rule Tail. This project reunited SePH, PuzzleDude, Euclid, and Conn, along with other community members, for a massive, decade-long overhaul of ALttP that turned it into a Conker-themed game. It was explicitly designed as a spiritual successor to Parallel Worlds and pushed the SNES hardware with new ASM for menus, HDMA effects, and MSU-1 audio support.

If you're aiming to emulate the Parallel Worlds style, studying these post-PW projects—especially Conker's High Rule Tail and Goddess of Wisdom—will give you a clearer picture of how this specific team evolved their design and technical approach over time.
